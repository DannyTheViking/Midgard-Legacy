# Bulk crafting and card stock

Added quantity selection to:

- Iron Bars
- Iron Nails
- Iron Axe Heads
- Iron Hoops
- Birch Planks
- Oak Planks

Each card now shows:

- Amount of the required material owned
- Maximum number of batches currently possible
- Amount of the produced item already owned

The Blacksmith cards show available silver, shafts,
axe heads and how many Iron Axes can currently be assembled.

Iron Axe assembly remains one at a time because it equips
the resulting axe directly into the single axe equipment slot.
