# Update 037 — Training Layout and Whole-Item Barter

- Total Warrior Stats now spans the full width beneath the Silver and Session Cost cards.
- The four trainable battle-stat cards remain in one row underneath.
- Trading previews now show 0 when an offer is worth less than one requested item.
- Trades always round down to whole items; fractional items are never granted.
- The trade button is disabled when the player lacks enough goods or the offer is too small.
